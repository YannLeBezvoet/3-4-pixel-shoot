Resurrect 64 palette

The Resurrect 64 palette (palette/resurrect-64.gpl) was created by Kerrie Lake and is
published on Lospec: https://lospec.com/palette-list/resurrect-64

It is a list of 64 colors, included in this repository so that the game's art and the palette
check use exactly the same colors. It is credited to its author in CREDITS.md. It is not covered
by the project's MIT or CC BY 4.0 licenses; see the source page for its author's terms.
